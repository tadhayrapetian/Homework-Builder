Good to know

Questions teachers ask first.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Worksheet Studio opens in your browser on a Mac, a PC or an iPad. On an iPad you can add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.

Where are my worksheets stored?

On your own device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Your work is saved automatically in the browser, and you can also save project files and open them again later.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, everything — the library, the editor and the PDF export — runs locally. Useful in a classroom with unreliable Wi-Fi.

Can I edit the ready-made exercises?

Every block you drop on the page is fully editable: change the sentences, add your own items, delete what you do not need, and reorder anything.

Is the answer key automatic?

Yes. Switch it on and the key is generated from the exercises on your page and added as a final sheet, with marks totalled if you have set them.

Can I put my school's name on the worksheets?

Yes. Set a footer once in the settings and it appears at the bottom of every page you print.
