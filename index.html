Questions

Good to know

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser. On a Mac just open the link. On an iPad, open it in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen to get an app icon that opens full-screen.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, everything — the editor, the grammar library, the dictionary and PDF export — runs on your own device with no connection.

Where are my worksheets stored?

On your device only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your work is autosaved in the browser, and you can also save a project file to keep or share.

Can I edit the ready-made exercises?

Every single sentence. The library gives you a filled-in starting point, not a locked template — rewrite anything, delete what you don't need, and use the + button to add as many extra items as you like.

Why does vector PDF matter?

Because the export is true vector, the text stays perfectly sharp however far you zoom in, it can be selected and searched, and the file stays small enough to e-mail. Image-based exports go blurry and heavy.

Which levels does the grammar library cover?

The library is split into Grammar (96 topics) and Vocabulary (20 topics), and each is graded A2, B1, B2 and C1 so you can jump straight to your group’s level.
